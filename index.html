FAQ

Common Questions

What does the free assessment include?
A full audit of your network topology, firewall config, endpoint security posture, cloud spend, and backup coverage. You get a written findings report with prioritized recommendations and honest cost estimates — no obligation, no sales pitch.
How is this different from a typical managed service provider?
Most MSPs assign tickets to tier-1 techs who escalate to tier-2 who call the vendor. You work directly with a CCNP-certified engineer who has designed these systems at enterprise scale. No ticket queue. No account manager buffer. The person you talk to fixes the problem.
What size businesses do you work with?
Primarily small to mid-size businesses across Southern California — typically 5 to 250 employees — who need enterprise-grade infrastructure but can't justify a full-time IT hire.
Do you also do residential smart home installation?
Yes. We design and install smart home systems for homeowners — lighting, locks, security cameras, climate, and AV integration. Book a free home consultation to get started.
Do you offer ongoing support or just project work?
Both. Project-based engagements (network redesign, cloud migration, security hardening, smart home install) and ongoing managed IT with proactive monitoring and automated remediation.
