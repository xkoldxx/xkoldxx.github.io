Common Questions

What does the free network assessment include?

A full audit of your network topology, firewall config, endpoint security posture, cloud spend, and backup coverage. You get a written findings report with prioritized recommendations and honest cost estimates — no upsell pressure, no sales pitch.

How is this different from a typical managed service provider?

Most MSPs assign a ticket to a tier-1 tech who escalates to tier-2 who escalates to a vendor. You work directly with a CCNP-certified engineer who has designed these systems at enterprise scale. No ticket queue. No account manager buffer. The person you talk to is the person who fixes it.

What size businesses do you work with?

Primarily small to mid-size businesses across Southern California — typically 5 to 250 employees — who need enterprise-grade infrastructure but can't justify a full-time IT hire. If you're outgrowing your current setup or just tired of things breaking, we're the right fit.

Can you work with our existing equipment?

Yes. The assessment starts with what you have. We recommend replacements only when existing gear is end-of-life, unsupported, or genuinely creating risk. If your Cisco switch is fine, we configure it — we don't sell you a new one.

Do you offer ongoing support or just project work?

Both. Project-based engagements (network redesign, cloud migration, security hardening) and ongoing managed IT with proactive monitoring and automated remediation. Most clients start with a project and move to managed once they see how the infrastructure runs.
