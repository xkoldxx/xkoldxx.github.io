For owner-run businesses with 3 to 25 people and no IT department. You get one person who knows your setup, explains things in plain English, and fixes it.

A Website That Brings in Customers

A fast, mobile-friendly site, set up so customers find you on Google. Fixed price, agreed up front, then kept updated so it never goes stale.

Email & Accounts, Sorted

Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace set up properly. New hires set up before their first day, departing staff's access removed cleanly, and no paying for licenses nobody uses.

Protection From the Bad Day

2-step login, protected laptops, and backups I've actually tested by restoring them. Skip one of these and a single phishing email can lock up the whole business.

Help When Something Breaks

Call or email the engineer who already knows your setup, so you never explain your business twice. Most fixes happen remotely without anyone leaving their desk, and updates and monitoring run in the background so fewer things break.

The Right Equipment

Laptops, wifi, and printers chosen for how your business works, not upsold. I order it and set it up remotely, and when a printer or laptop stops cooperating, most fixes happen over a screen share. If it's a broken part, I'll tell you plainly what to ask the repair shop.

Use AI Without Leaking Your Data

ChatGPT, Copilot, and Gemini can save your team real time, but free versions may keep what you paste in. I set up the right business plans and privacy settings, give staff a one-page "what's OK to paste" policy, and automate the busywork: drafting replies, summarizing email, sorting inquiries.

FAQ

Common Questions

What happens on the free IT review?
A 15-minute video call. I look at your email and accounts, backups, website, AI tool use, and how your team gets support today. You get a one-page written list of what's costing you money or about to break. No obligation.
Can you really support my business remotely?
Yes. Almost everything a small business needs (accounts, email, security, backups, websites, laptops, network settings) is managed through secure remote tools. Nothing is accessed without your permission.
Is it safe to let you into our computers remotely?
I only connect when you've asked for help or approved planned work, using my own named login with 2-step verification, never a shared password. Your business keeps ownership of its accounts and admin passwords, and you can remove my access at any time.
Is it safe for my staff to use ChatGPT or Copilot?
It can be, with the right setup. The risk usually isn't the tool. It's staff pasting customer or company information into a free personal account nobody checked. I move you to business plans with the right data controls, write a one-page policy your team will actually read, and show you which tasks are worth automating.
What if something needs a person on site?
I don't do on-site work. Most small business problems don't need a visit. When something physical needs doing, like plugging in a new device, I'll walk you or a staff member through it on a video call. If it truly needs a technician in the room, I'll tell you plainly and give you a written list of exactly what to ask them for.
You're one person. What if you're unavailable?
Fair question. If I'm away, you'll hear about it ahead of time whenever possible. If I'm unexpectedly out, the written guide in your own account tells you where everything is and how to get in, so you or any IT provider can act right away without waiting for me. You're never locked in.
Do you charge by the hour?
Website projects are a fixed price agreed up front. Ongoing support is a flat monthly plan with a set amount of time included, and you approve anything beyond it before it's billed. Engineering work is quoted per engagement.
Do you work with in-house IT teams?
Yes. Network, systems, cloud, and automation engineering on a project or contract basis: firewall and routing work, server and identity administration, migrations, and Ansible automation.
Who will I actually talk to?
The engineer doing the work. I'm one person, and everything I set up is documented in your own accounts.
Am I locked into a long contract?
No long-term lock-in. After the free review you get the monthly price in writing before you agree to anything.
